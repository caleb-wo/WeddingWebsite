# Purpose

This site was used during June, July, & August of 2025. I got married in August & we used this site for our friends & family to visit to see pictures, RSVP, get our registry, see the schedule, & more. As you explore the project, you'll see that I was unable to write tests—this is because of the time crunch I was on trying to get it out. Even so, it is a simple site & we were able to save some money thanks to it! I'm really proud of it. Although it's _very_ simply & I had to cut some corners to make my deadline, it was an inredible experience to make a piece of software that serviced my friends & family with no issues.

## Design

It is a Spring Framework full stack application. In the project, I also have a collection of WebP photos from my engangement pictures. The frontend makes a call to the backend & gets the images needed as the user goes through each page. I utilized Heroku to host the site. On our wedding invites we included a QR code that pointed to the heroku web address. We also used a custom TinyUrl—_tinyurl.com/mrxmrswolfe_—instead of purchasing a domain name as a more accesible option for those who could not use the QR code.
